Window sizes

Set the minimum and maximum for each slider, in millimetres. The slider and its number field use the same limits.

The grid needs at least 450 mm per window. In a multi-window layout, geometry may raise the overall minimum or further restrict individual resizing.

These are slider limits, not manufacturing approvals. The existing 2,500 mm per-window cart limit and 130 kg opening-sash weight check are unchanged.
