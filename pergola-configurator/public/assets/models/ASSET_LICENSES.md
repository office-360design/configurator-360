# 3D asset licenses

The GLB files in this directory are original low-poly assets created for this pergola demo and are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**. They may be copied, modified and redistributed without attribution.

The environmental art direction was selected to be compatible with these external CC0 references:

- Kenney Nature Kit — https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- Kenney City Kit (Suburban) — https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban

No external binary model is hot-linked at runtime; all models used by the configurator are stored locally so the demo remains deterministic and works offline after dependencies are installed.
