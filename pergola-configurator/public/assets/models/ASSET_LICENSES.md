# 3D asset licenses

All GLB files bundled in this directory are original lightweight demo assets created for this configurator and released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**. They may be copied, modified and redistributed without attribution.

Included assets:

- environment/house.glb
- environment/tree.glb
- accessories/hand-crank.glb
- accessories/wall-switch.glb
- accessories/outlet-eu.glb
- accessories/outlet-us.glb
- accessories/heater.glb
- accessories/spotlight.glb
- accessories/rain-sensor.glb
- accessories/wind-sensor.glb
- accessories/speaker.glb
- accessories/led-strip.glb
- accessories/screen-cassette.glb

The procedural source for the revised demo models is included at:

```text
tools/generate_demo_assets.py
```

The environmental art direction and licensing approach were checked against these public-domain libraries:

- Kenney Nature Kit — https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- Kenney City Kit (Suburban) — https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban
- Poly Haven license — https://polyhaven.com/license

No third-party binary is hot-linked or downloaded at runtime. Keeping the GLBs local makes the demo deterministic, lightweight and usable offline after JavaScript dependencies are installed.

The `public/assets/360CONFIGURATOR.png` brand image was supplied by the project owner and is not covered by the CC0 statement above.
