Bookshelf lower-door structural update

Implemented:
- Lower solid doors now occupy the opening between shelf 1 and shelf 4.
- The bottom edge of the door opening aligns to the top of shelf 1.
- The top edge of the door opening aligns to the underside of shelf 4.
- Shelves 2 and 3 are shortened in depth when lower doors are selected so they no longer poke through the door plane.
- Lower solid doors now use a framed construction: two vertical stiles, top and bottom rails, and an inset centre panel.
- The doors are positioned in the front frame plane instead of floating in front of the module.

Notes:
- This update focuses on the lower solid-door geometry only.
- Glazed doors were left unchanged.
